# Game Concept

## Title (working)
**Kindred Wilds**

## One-line Pitch
A child and their creature companion explore a living wilderness — gathering food, growing together, and uncovering the secrets of evolution.

## Genre
2D top-down exploration / creature nurturing

## Tone
Warm, curious, low-stakes. No violence. Discovery-driven.

---

## Premise
You play as a child at the edge of a wild region. A local keeper offers you a creature companion to raise. Together you explore the wilderness, gather food to feed and grow your creature, find rare items, and witness your companion evolve into something new.

The world is small but alive. Each area tells a quiet story. The protagonist and creature grow together.

---

## Core Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Bond** | The player nurtures one creature. Growth feels earned, not automated. |
| **Explore** | Regions are hand-crafted. Secrets reward curiosity. |
| **Evolve** | Evolution requires effort (leveling) and discovery (finding the item). It is a moment, not a menu option. |
| **Story** | Short text events mark progress. The world reacts to what you have done. |

---

## Player Fantasy
"I found something rare, fed it to my creature, and watched it become something I had never seen before."

---

## Target Audience
- Ages 10+
- Fans of creature-nurturing games
- Players who enjoy low-pressure exploration
- Players who want story and atmosphere over combat

---

## Platform Target
- Desktop (Windows / macOS / Linux) via Godot 4 export
- Web export (itch.io) for easy sharing
